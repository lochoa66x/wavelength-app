# Frozen-scope backlog

The following known findings are retained without changing them in this cycle:
- Formulaic or underdeveloped cover letters.
- Repeated skills and qualifications, including First Aid in multiple sections.
- Uneven résumé tense and weak graduate positioning.
- Independent masked human review and a real-user value pilot.
- Existing frontend bundle-size warning.

Reopen scope only for a concrete factual-corruption defect or a problem that prevents use. General writing preferences do not automatically trigger another implementation cycle.
